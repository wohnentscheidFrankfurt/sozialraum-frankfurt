# Land values 1.1.2026 on the zones of 2024

Question of the user (20.10.2026): BORIS shows 3,300 €/m² in the Ostend, the map showed 4,200. Why?

## Answer

- The map used the Stichtag 1.1.2024, BORIS shows 1.1.2026. Zone 43391012: 3,900 (2024), 3,300 (2026). [belegt:
  `sources/hessen/boris/boris_01.gml`, WMS `hboris_feature`]
- The map shows one median per area (cells weighted by residents), BORIS one zone. The zones of the Ostend reach
  from 950 to 7,200 €/m² (2024). [eigene Auswertung]
- With the values of 2026 the Ostend reads 3,600 €/m². [eigene Auswertung]
- The Ostend fell by about 15 %. The city as a whole did not: of 762 zones, 307 fell by more than 5 %, 260 changed by
  −5 % to 0, 195 rose. The median ratio 2026 to 2024 is 0.99. [eigene Auswertung]
- The city median of the map, weighted by residents, falls from 1,500 to 1,300 €/m². Many residents live in zones
  that fell. [eigene Auswertung]

## Source facts

- The BORIS WFS exists for 2020, 2022 and 2024 only. `brw/2026/wfs` answers 404 (20.10.2026). The Geoportal Hessen
  (GovData „BORIS Hessen“) registers WFS 731, 732 and 864: 2022, 2020, 2024. [belegt]
- The WMS of HVBG and of the city (`geowebdienste.frankfurt.de/BRW2026`) give values per point, no geometry. [belegt]
- Both services allow every use without condition (AccessConstraints): dl-de/zero-2-0. [belegt]

## Zones of 2024 against 2026

- `scripts/boris_2026_zone_match.py`, three points per zone of 2024 (inner point and two random inner points):
  of 765 residential and mixed zones, 755 keep their number at all three points, 8 at some, 2 at none.
  Result: `results/boris_2026_zone_match.csv`. [eigene Auswertung]
- The map rule (one inner point per zone, same number) drops 3 zones: 43300413, 41190598, 41300835. [eigene
  Auswertung]

## Built values against a query of every cell

`scripts/boris_2026_cells.py` asks the WMS at the centre of every inner cell; `scripts/boris_2026_compare.py` compares
with the build (`results/boris_2026_compare.txt`):

- 6,058 inner cells have a value in both. 6 of them differ (771 residents), where a border moved and the zone kept
  its number.
- All 45 Stadtteile and the city keep their median. One of 114 districts differs: 252, 3,500 instead of 3,600 €/m².

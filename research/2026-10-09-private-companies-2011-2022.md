# Private companies as owners: Zensus 2011 and 2022 compared

As of 9 Oct 2026. Sources in `sources/05 Eigentümerstruktur/`:

- `Zensus2022_4000W-1011_Frankfurt_2011_2022.csv`: dwellings in buildings with residential space by ownership type of the building, Zensus 2011 and 2022, territory of 15 May 2022.
- `Zensus2022_3000G-2001_Frankfurt_2011_2022.csv`: buildings with residential space by year of construction (decades) and ownership type, same base.
- `Zensus2022_Metadaten_Eigentumsform.json`: definition of the variable GEBEG1, the variables of both tables and the list of tables of statistic 4000W.
- `Zensus2022_4000W-2002_Frankfurt.csv`: the source of the map.
- `statistik_aktuell_2013-11_GWZ_2011_Frankfurt.pdf`: brochure "Gebäude- und Wohnungszählung 2011 in Frankfurt am Main: Erste Ergebnisse auf einen Blick".
- `wemgehoertdiestadt_frankfurt_page-data.json`.

The extracts were made with `analyses/zensus_db_extract.py`.

Labels: [belegt] = printed in the source; [eigene Auswertung] = counted by us; [unsicher]; [offen].

## Question

The map shows 18.6 % for "Private Unternehmen" (city value). The brochure of 2011 gives a lower share. "Wem gehört die Stadt?" gives 6 % for "Finanzmarkt und Börse". Where does the difference come from?

## 1. The map is close to the Zensus 2022

- The Zensus 2022 counts 57,076 dwellings of private housing companies and 19,264 dwellings of other private companies. [belegt]
- Together that is 76,340 of 402,826 dwellings, or 19.0 %. [eigene Auswertung]
- The map estimates the share from the 100 m grid and gets 18.57 %. The grid assigns an ownership type to 97.05 % of the dwellings. Both values are in `interim/city_values.csv` of the build (`OWN_COMPANY`, `COV_OWN`). [eigene Auswertung]
- The gap of 0.4 percentage points comes from the estimate on the grid. [eigene Auswertung]

## 2. The share rose from 10.4 % to 19.0 %

Same base in both years: dwellings in buildings with residential space, territory of 2022, final values of 2011. Both years use the same content variable WHG002. [belegt]

| Dwellings | 2011 | 2022 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private housing companies | 27,770 | 57,076 | +29,306 |
| Other private companies | 10,077 | 19,264 | +9,187 |
| **Private companies together** | **37,847 (10.4 %)** | **76,340 (19.0 %)** | **+38,493** |
| Private persons | 131,689 | 134,116 | +2,427 |
| Owners' associations (WEG) | 101,464 | 98,759 | −2,705 |
| Municipality or municipal company | 52,189 | 69,558 | +17,369 |
| Federation or Land | 20,461 | 5,651 | −14,810 |
| Cooperatives | 13,390 | 12,932 | −458 |
| Organisations without profit motive | 8,359 | 5,471 | −2,888 |
| All dwellings | 365,401 | 402,826 | +37,425 |

Single values [belegt]; sums, shares and changes [eigene Auswertung].

- The parts do not add up exactly to the total: 365,399 in 2011, 402,827 in 2022. The Zensus anonymises each cell on its own. [eigene Auswertung]
- The share grew by 8.6 percentage points, by a factor of 1.8. The number of dwellings grew by a factor of 2.0. [eigene Auswertung]
- The brochure of 2013 gives 28,029 + 9,147 = 37,176 dwellings. That is 10.5 % of 354,676 dwellings. [belegt; sum and share eigene Auswertung]
- The brochure counts only residential buildings ("Alle nachfolgenden Betrachtungen beziehen sich auf die Wohngebäude") and uses preliminary values ("vorläufige Ergebnisse des Zensus 2011"). [belegt]
- The two values for 2011 differ by 0.1 percentage points. The base is therefore not the cause. The difference is a change between the two counts. [eigene Auswertung]

## 3. For private companies, most of the increase lies in older buildings

Buildings with residential space (content variable GEB001), table 3000G-2001. [belegt]

The columns "2011" and "2022" are the printed totals. The columns "before 2010" and "from 2010" are sums of the decade cells. Cell sums differ from the totals by up to 9 buildings, because of the anonymisation. [eigene Auswertung]

| Buildings | 2011 | 2022 | Change | Change, built before 2010 | Change, built from 2010 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Private companies | 4,522 | 6,796 | +2,274 | +1,741 | +535 (76 → 611) |
| Private persons | 47,290 | 50,138 | +2,848 | −266 | +3,125 |
| Owners' associations (WEG) | 13,088 | 12,146 | −942 | −2,107 | +1,165 |
| Municipality | 7,140 | 8,508 | +1,368 | +947 | +411 |
| Federation or Land | 2,507 | 577 | −1,930 | −1,955 | +15 |
| All | 77,457 | 80,679 | +3,222 | −2,090 | +5,319 |

"From 2010" holds the decade 2010–2015 and the years from 2016. In 2011 this class held only buildings of 2010 and early 2011. [eigene Auswertung]

- For all owners together, the growth is new construction. The stock built before 2010 shrank by 2,090 buildings. [eigene Auswertung]
- For private companies it is the other way round. They gained 1,741 buildings built before 2010 and 535 buildings built from 2010. That is 77 % of their growth in older buildings. [eigene Auswertung]
- The largest gains of private companies are in buildings from 1950–1959 (+672) and 1960–1969 (+402). [eigene Auswertung]
- Private companies own 611 of the 5,625 buildings built from 2010. That is 11 %. [eigene Auswertung]
- In the older stock, "Federation or Land" lost 1,955 buildings and WEG lost 2,107. The municipality gained 947. [eigene Auswertung]
- The Zensus 2022 lists the Nassauische Heimstätte under "municipality" (spec `docs/specs/2026-10-06-interne-karte-design.md`, section 18). This moves buildings from "Federation or Land" to "municipality". The number of Nassauische Heimstätte buildings is not known. [belegt; number offen]
- On the printed totals, "Federation or Land" lost 1,930 buildings and the municipality gained 1,368. At least 562 buildings of "Federation or Land" therefore went to other owners or were demolished. [eigene Auswertung]
- The Zensus cannot tell whether a building was sold or only classified differently in 2022. Part of the shift may be a different classification. [unsicher]
- The table list of statistic 4000W has no table with dwellings by year of construction and ownership type at municipal level (`Zensus2022_Metadaten_Eigentumsform.json`). The share of private companies in new dwellings is therefore not known. [belegt; share offen]

## 4. "Wem gehört die Stadt?" uses other groups

- Frankfurt: Finanzmarkt und Börse 6 %, Großes Privateigentum 19 %, Kleines Privateigentum 29 %, Selbstnutzer:innen 20 %, Genossenschaft und Gemeinnützig 7 %, Staat 20 %. [belegt, page-data JSON]
- "Finanzmarkt und Börse" covers funds, banks, insurers and listed housing companies. It is a part of the Zensus group "private companies". Family companies (GmbH) count as "Großes Privateigentum" there (see `Wohnungsmarkt_Sozialraum_Protokoll.md`, section 4). [belegt]
- The basis is the count of 2011, carried forward and estimated, marked "Beta". [belegt]

## 5. Definition in the Zensus

From the variable GEBEG1 in `Zensus2022_Metadaten_Eigentumsform.json` [belegt]:

- "Privatwirtschaftliches Wohnungsunternehmen: Alle privatrechtlichen Wohnungsunternehmen, ohne Genossenschaften."
- "Anderes privatwirtschaftliches Unternehmen: Alle privatrechtlichen Unternehmen, in deren Eigentum sich Wohnungen befinden, deren primärer Erwerbszweck aber nicht die Wohnungsvermietung ist (beispielsweisen Banken, Versicherungen, Fonds)."
- "Das Merkmal bildet ab, wem das Eigentum an dem Gebäude (nicht der einzelnen Wohnung) rechtlich zusteht."

## Consequences

- The map stays as it is. The layer keeps the name "Private Unternehmen" (user decision 9 Oct 2026). A name like "Vonovia & Co" would suggest large listed companies only.
- The ⓘ of "Private Unternehmen" now names the Zensus definition in short form.
- The map file shows these numbers in the view "Eigentum 2011–2022" (spec `docs/specs/2026-10-09-ownership-history-design.md`).
- Covered for political statements: "Laut Zensus gehörten 2011 10,4 % der Wohnungen privaten Unternehmen, 2022 waren es 19,0 %." [belegt]
- Not covered: "wurde verkauft". Part of the change may be a different classification. [eigene Einschätzung]
- Buildings against dwellings: see findings/2026-10-10-buildings-and-dwellings-by-owner.md.
